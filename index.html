DWG NO. 001 — UNIVERSITY

IC Wrench

SOFTWARE
Fusion 360 (CAD + CAM)
PROCESS
3-axis CNC milling
OUTPUT
Machined, functional part

Modeled a combination wrench from scratch in Fusion 360, then moved into the CAM workspace to program the toolpaths for machining — setting operations, feeds and speeds, and tool selection for the full sequence.

Machined the part on a CNC mill, taking it from a solid model on screen to a finished piece of tooling. The project tied together design intent, tolerancing, and what's actually achievable at the machine.

DWG NO. 002 — UNIVERSITY

Hydroponics System

CONTROL
Arduino, C/C++
HARDWARE
Water pump + mount
FABRICATION
3D printed bracket

Wrote the Arduino code that controls the water pump for a hydroponics setup, handling the logic that drives watering cycles for the plants.

Designed and 3D printed a mounting bracket to hold the pump in place within the system — a small part, but one that had to fit the pump exactly and hold up to being wet most of the time.

DWG NO. 003 — HIGH SCHOOL

Soap Box Derby Car

PROCESS
Manual machining
SETTING
High school shop
OUTPUT
Competition-ready car

Manually machined the components of a soap box derby car — my first real time behind a mill and lathe, cutting to fit, checking tolerances by hand, and learning what it actually takes to turn stock material into a finished part.

This project is where the shop habits started: measure twice, respect the tool, and expect the first pass to teach you something the drawing didn't.
